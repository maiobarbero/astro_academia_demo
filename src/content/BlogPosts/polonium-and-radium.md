---
title: "From pitchblende to polonium and radium"
date: "1898-12-26"
excerpt: "How measurements of uranium ore residues led Marie and Pierre Curie to report two new radioactive elements in 1898."
tags: ["radioactivity", "chemistry", "history"]
---

Pitchblende remained unusually active after uranium was removed. Marie and Pierre Curie followed that clue through repeated measurements and chemical separations. In July 1898, they reported a new substance they named polonium; in December, they and Gustave Bémont reported evidence for radium.

Identifying the elements was only an early step. Isolating radium in a form pure enough to study required years of processing large quantities of ore. Their work joined physical measurement with demanding chemical analysis.

**Historical note:** This is a modern summary of the research, not a text written by the Curies.
