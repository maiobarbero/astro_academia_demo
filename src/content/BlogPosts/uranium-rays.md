---
title: "Measuring the rays from uranium"
date: "1898-04-12"
excerpt: "Marie Curie’s first report on radiation from uranium and thorium compounds, and the careful measurements that shaped her research."
tags: ["radioactivity", "physics"]
---

In April 1898, Marie Curie presented a short communication to the French Academy of Sciences on rays emitted by uranium and thorium compounds. She measured their effect with an electrical method using a piezoelectric quartz apparatus developed with Pierre Curie.

The measurements suggested that the activity was tied to the substances themselves. This work gave Curie a way to compare samples quantitatively and led her to investigate minerals that proved more active than uranium alone.

**Historical note:** This is a modern summary of the research, not a text written by Curie.
